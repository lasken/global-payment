The Process

Simple. Fast.
No Wahala.

From sign-up to naira in your account in under 5 minutes. Here's exactly how Global Payment works for you.

01
Create Your Global Payment Account
Sign up in under 2 minutes. Verify your BVN and identity to unlock full trading access. We take compliance seriously so your funds are always safe.
02
Deposit or Transfer Crypto
Send BTC or ETH to your unique Global Payment wallet address. We support all major wallets. Your balance shows up in real time.
03
Set Your Rate or Use Best Market
Choose to sell instantly at current best P2P rate or set a target rate and we match you to the highest bidder, automatically.
04
Receive Naira Instantly
Once matched, naira hits your registered bank account fast. No delays, no chasing buyers, no trust issues. Just money moving.
05

Don't want to sell yet? Park your BTC or ETH in your Global Payment wallet and participate in our yield pool, your crypto works while you sleep.
